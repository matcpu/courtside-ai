## 0:00–0:14 — Founder motivation

I'm a youth basketball coach. I've seen seasons become a scramble: unanswered emails, cancelled gyms, and confusion about who should play where. So I built the system I wish we'd had.

## 0:14–0:44 — Starting the season

It starts before the first tip-off. A coach tells COURTSIDE what their team actually looks like: previous results, experience, and where they think they belong. Instead of endless back-and-forth, the league gets a consistent starting point. Meta's Muse Spark can identify contradictions and suggest questions to ask, while the league makes the final placement decision.

## 0:44–1:13 — Age verification and fair play

And fairness matters. We've all heard about age eligibility disputes that can overshadow kids' games. With COURTSIDE, guardians can securely upload proof of age directly to the league. The system screens for possible inconsistencies and puts the original document in front of an administrator. It helps leagues identify issues earlier, while keeping people responsible for verification.

## 1:13–1:32 — Courts and scheduling

Then comes the scheduling chaos. A gym changes availability. A team isn't ready. Suddenly, ten different people need answers. COURTSIDE brings courts, team readiness, and scheduling together, helping organizers build workable fixtures and communicate approved changes.

## 1:32–2:01 — Scoresheet intelligence

After every game, there's usually more paperwork. Instead of chasing scoresheets and manually entering results, a coach simply uploads a photo. Muse Spark reads the scoresheet, extracts the results, and shows the league what it found. Once confirmed, the official result feeds into the standings.

## 2:01–2:36 — The AI operations agent

But here's where it gets really powerful. As scores come in, COURTSIDE can identify when divisions are becoming unbalanced. The Meta-powered agent goes beyond simply offering advice. It selects tools to investigate results, check team history and readiness, recommend moves, and prepare a revised schedule. In this demonstration, the administrator approved two team moves. The revised schedule remains a draft until publication is authorized. The agent prepares the work. The league makes the decisions.

## 2:36–2:50 — The vision

I built this so kids get fairer games, coaches get clear communication, and volunteers get their time back. From preseason to the final buzzer. This is COURTSIDE AI.
